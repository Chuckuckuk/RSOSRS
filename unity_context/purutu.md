Pugnacious Purutu is a 2-phase encounter beginning with Purutu, a large gorilla infused with shadow energy.

# Phase 1: Purutu

Purutu himself is a simple fight. He is a blocking 3x3 enemy, preventing step-unders and run-skipping his corner tiles. Purutu will attack with melee in melee range, or traditional ranged at a distance; Purutu's ranged attacks are accurate and have high prayer penetration, encouraging the player to stay near him. He will attempt to approach at all times despite being capable of ranged attacks. Taking any damage from Purutu's special attacks will apply Corruption to the player, draining xx prayer points over xx ticks. 

## Special Attacks

### Ground Smash

Purutu prepares to strike an area in front of him. After a brief delay, he smashes the area around his target, damaging anyone standing in the area.

### Slicer

Purutu conjures up a levitating orb that follows a random target. This orb pulses for damage around itself and gains duration when it lands a hit.

# Phase 2: Fire/Ice/Electric Boogaloo

The 3 elemental gorillas (Irritable Igniscia, Annoyed Aguascia, and Agitated Aeriscia) spawn in. 

## Fire

Chases at running speed, mostly attacking with melee. May queue up a magic attack that lands after a delay.
May attempt to slam or charge. Both leave temporary fire on affected tiles.
Taking any avoidable damage from Igniscia applies a 5-damage burn.

## Lightning

Chases and attacks similarly to Igniscia. Waits to attack until after him. May queue up a ranged attack that lands more quickly than igniscia. Uses his ranged less often.
Likes to charge. Taking avoidable damage from Aeriscia disables running for nn ticks.

## Ice

Attempts to remain at a distance and mostly attacks with a slow ice ball AoE attack. This stuns any affected player for nn ticks. Also may use a magic attack which lands after a delay.
