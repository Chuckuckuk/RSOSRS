Researched (via a subagent surveying oldschool.runescape.wiki across GWD/Zulrah-Vorkath tier/Raids 1-3/wilderness bosses) a 16-category taxonomy of structurally distinct boss mechanics, to avoid over-fitting the combat architecture to TzTok-Jad's shape (single telegraphed attack + binary prayer check + one HP-threshold spawn). See [[project-bidirectional-interactions]] for the earlier related decision that interaction requests must be actor-agnostic.

**Why:** the user's explicit goal is a system modular enough that "anyone capable of translating attack patterns into NPC behaviors could recreate or create any fight or encounter possible" — not just Jad. Building to Jad's shape alone would have required reworking the architecture once a second, structurally different boss (e.g. Verzik's phase-swapped movesets, or Vorkath's fixed deterministic rotation) was attempted.

**Categories identified** (full detail in conversation; condensed here): room/radius AoE hitting all occupants; prayer-piercing specials (**refined** - see [[project-osrs-damage-calc-research]]: "pierce" (partial prayer penetration, e.g. Phantom Muspah's ranged attacks) and "ignore" (100% penetration, e.g. Doom of Mokhaiotl's ground slams) are different points on one spectrum, not interchangeable - K'ril's ignore and Callisto's ~50% pierce were two different mechanics even in the original research, don't collapse them into one bool); forced-movement/tile-avoidance reactions; shared/split damage; fixed deterministic rotations; time/counter-conditional rotations; HP-threshold phase swaps that replace the whole moveset/arena (not just spawn adds); linked multi-NPC state (one entity's HP/status changes another's vulnerability); hard DPS-check/burn-timer windows; healing/resource-denial mechanics distinct from kill-the-healer spawns; mandatory non-attack object/environment interaction (Wintertodt, Zalcano); interrupt-via-correct-targeting; positioning relative to other players/entities; instance-wide ambient hazards independent of the boss's own attack roll; multi-combat/PvP-risk layers (wilderness bosses); group-scaling/contribution-based loot.

**Follow-up pass** (Doom of Mokhaiotl, Phantom Muspah, Vardorvis, Yama, TzKal-Zuk, Sol Heredit, Corrupted Gauntlet) ruled out several suspected categories after verifying against the live wiki text (Muspah is a fixed single-instance fight, not open-world roaming; Zuk's "pillars" are actually a moving shield construct, not physical cover; Vardorvis's bind and the Gauntlet's style-swap both reduce to existing categories) and surfaced four more genuinely new ones:
- **A. Escalating multi-encounter run with bankable/forfeitable rewards** — a sequence of discrete encounters with rising difficulty (auto-scaling or player-chosen debuff "modifiers"), where accumulated loot is claimable by stopping but fully forfeited on death/early exit. (Doom of Mokhaiotl, Sol Heredit/Fortis Colosseum)
- **B. HP-reactive special-attack frequency** — pure weighted-random attack selection where an HP threshold shifts only the *weights*, not the moveset itself (distinct from a wholesale phase swap). (Vardorvis)
- **C. Player-style-reactive positioning AI** — the NPC's own movement/engagement behavior (close distance vs. kite) is driven by which combat style the player is currently using, not by a timer or HP threshold. (Yama)
- **D. Finite-capacity shared mitigation construct** — a shield/barrier object with its own HP-like budget and independent movement, depleted by secondary sources (not the boss's main attack) rather than requiring a kill to stop it; once exhausted it's gone for the rest of the fight. (TzKal-Zuk's Ancestral Glyph)

Confirmed out of scope: the Gauntlet's pre-fight resource-gathering prep room (non-combat gear-gating, not an attack-behavior concern).

**How to apply:** when designing the combat/encounter data model, prioritize (in order): (1) shapes beyond point/radius for `TargetSpec`-style attack-hit-detection (cone, line); (2) `Phase` as a first-class object bundling moveset+hazards+vulnerability+**movement policy** (per category C), swapped wholesale on HP/timer/event triggers, not just an HP-threshold spawn hook; (3) a shared Encounter/Room context object that multiple NPCs and hazards **and generic sub-entities with their own resource pool** (per category D) read/write — build this *before* generalizing the player's mover into a generic `Actor`, since Actor needs a place to plug into shared state, and `Actor` should be general enough to cover non-combat shield/barrier objects, not just "things that fight"; (4) a generic `ReactionRequirement` on attacks (PrayerMatch / MoveToTile / Interrupt / None) instead of hardcoding prayer checks; (5) a deadline+condition+consequence primitive for DPS checks/enrage timers, separate from the telegraph-resolve state machine; (6) attack-pattern selection weights should be a function of live state (HP%, per category B), not just swapped at phase boundaries. Explicitly out of scope: object/environment-interaction minigames (Wintertodt/Zalcano/Gauntlet prep room), group-scaling/loot-contribution (reward-layer, not attack-behavior), and the run/escalation wrapper layer (category A) — the latter is fully out of scope, not just deferred, per [[project-scope-moment-to-moment-only]]: nothing needs to persist between fights, so Encounter does not need to be designed for cross-fight chainability.
